DMT / dexmaxai airdrop phishing

Wiki id dmt-dexmaxai-airdrop-phishing-nov-2025 · Live incident page

Summary

On 20 Nov 2025 GoPlus warned that the @dexmaxai DMT airdrop tricked users into extra token authorizations. Pattern on Solana: Tokenkeg Approve then TransferChecked. 1,000+ wallets; >$130k bridged. Project X/site went offline (rug reporting).

On-chain evidence

Coverage gap. Truncated collector pubkeys and unverified HitBTC deposit attribution are not expanded to full hashes here. Do not invent a Tokenkeg approve/transfer signature list or a HitBTC hop. Wiki $130k remains an unhashed aggregate.

Root cause

Claim UI asked for a second signature that was SPL delegate approval, not the airdrop mint. Drainers then TransferChecked against the delegate.

Response

GoPlus: revoke delegates or move assets. Solana revoke tools, not EVM Revoke.cash.

Coverage gaps

  • Collector full pubkey / approve+transfer signature list.
  • HitBTC deposit hashes (unverified in this draft).

Upbit Solana hot wallet

Wiki id upbit-sol-hot-wallet-nov-2025 · Live incident page

Summary

Official Upbit notice: abnormal Solana hot-wallet withdrawals detected at 04:42 KST on 27 Nov 2025, sized at KRW 44.5 billion (corrected from KRW 54B using prices at that timestamp). Wiki ~$37M is a separate press estimate. This draft does not invent a USD conversion of the KRW print.

On-chain evidence

Notice 5800 lists destination accounts (excerpt of hashed SOL notice destinations; full table is on Upbit):

  • 2bhVZDJjRLTdJJZWtCC25t3bDY3iXQP7LXY2oQtRJJTA (SOL; also DRIFT ATA)
  • 33jzE31DDMF1cPdoLw8o8v5fiiiSTTyTSKrKRUJyRWWg (SOL; BONK / 2Z ATAs)
  • 5dorac4RBEp1ht2yNsfq6tEgmRqYoFsmnyrty8mqLYWc (SOL; BONK / JUP ATAs)
  • 8VWiu28Hvy74paVJhDsdxMx5mGAgy8W3qafNZAwpfTUy (SOL; JUP ATA)
  • CfjGxhkx9SvNATeGQySghf1j9DvoNmnuk6JWZMEPGjLc (SOL; JUP ATA 23JPm15PgYWV5uTnjADEVN8uSma2rdcULq3EGciTArzS)

Subsequent Jupiter aggregator dumps of stolen SPL into stables are widely reported (thousands of txs). This draft stops at notice destinations / Jupiter dumps without inventing dump signatures.

Coverage gap. Individual hot-wallet outflow signatures and Jupiter dump signatures are not hashed here. Do not convert KRW 44.5B into a USD figure in this draft.

Root cause

Unauthorized control of the Solana hot-wallet signing path. Cold wallets were reported untouched. Not a Solana L1 bug.

Response

Deposits/withdrawals paused; remaining hot assets to cold storage; Upbit pledged to cover customer losses from reserves. LAYER freeze (~KRW 2.3B in the corrected notice) via issuer cooperation.

Coverage gaps

  • Per-token outflow signatures from the hot wallet.
  • Hashed Jupiter dump txs (notice destinations only).

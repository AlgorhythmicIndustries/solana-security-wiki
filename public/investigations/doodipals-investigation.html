DoodiPals key leak

Wiki id doodipals-key-leak-oct-2025 · Live incident page

Summary

20–21 Oct 2025 (wiki date 21 Oct): leaked project keys let an attacker sell $DOODi from many wallets into SOL (~917 SOL / ~$171k reporting). Team and MistTrack traced SOL to FixedFloat. Distinct from a protocol vault bug.

On-chain evidence

Shared hot 5ndLnEYqSFiA5yUFHo6LVZ1eWc6Rhh11K5CfJNkoHEPs appears as a gas-funder cluster that also funds Across-related activity in later labeling. Treat that overlap as a FLAG, not a merge, without more evidence that DoodiPals and Across share an operator.

Coverage gap. Individual $DOODi→SOL sale signatures and FixedFloat deposit hashes are not in the sources used here. evilcos / MistTrack state the ~917 SOL outcome without a hashed sale list in this draft.

Root cause

Leaked private keys across many project wallets, not an AMM invariant failure.

Response

Team posted wallet/tx summaries and said they were working with FixedFloat; police report. Later 1:1 token migration talk for pre-hack holders.

Coverage gaps

  • Hashed SOL sales into FixedFloat.
  • Operator identity linking 5ndLnEYq to Across (FLAG only).

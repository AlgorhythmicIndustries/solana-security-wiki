BONKfun DNS hijack

Wiki id bonkfun-dns-mar-2026 · Live incident page

Summary

On 11 Mar 2026 the bonk.fun domain was hijacked after a team account compromise. A fake terms-of-service prompt ran a wallet drainer. The team said this was front-end / registrar abuse, not a BONK or Raydium program bug. Wiki lists ~$30k user losses and 110% compensation; control restored ~18–19 Mar.

On-chain evidence

Coverage gap / no protocol vault drain. No hashed collector, drainer program, or victim signature is in the public sources used here. Do not file this as a protocol vault drain unless a full hash is added later. Individual user-loss claims (including a $273k anecdote in press) are unhashed.

Root cause

Compromised team / registrar access that let attackers serve a malicious UI on the real domain. Users who signed the fake TOS authorized outbound transfers.

Response

Official X warning not to interact; domain recovery; pledged compensation. Wiki marks mitigated.

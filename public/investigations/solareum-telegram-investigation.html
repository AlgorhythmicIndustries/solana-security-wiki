Solareum Telegram

Wiki id solareum-telegram-mar-2024 · Live incident page

Not Solar X 2026. This is the March 2024 Solareum Telegram-bot key incident. Do not merge it with solar-solana-zh-x-jan-2026.

Summary

Users who imported keys into Solareum (including keys exported from BONKbot) were drained on 29 Mar 2024. Collector 6AZpMspmfdpA7jKCzHpypy18szmgh9sPTvxz6vcPY22a received +98.642084385 SOL from 29 wallets between 08:34–08:37 UTC. This is one of several 29 Mar collector clusters (tayvano list); later US reporting attributed a related intrusion to DPRK IT workers.

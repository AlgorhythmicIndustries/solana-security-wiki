NoOnes bridge

Wiki id noones-bridge-jan-2025 · Live incident page

Summary

P2P marketplace NoOnes: Solana bridge / hot-wallet logic abused from 1 Jan 2025 with many sub-$7k outflows across ETH, TRON, Solana, and BSC (~$7.9–8M reporting). CEO later confirmed a Solana-bridge exploit. Listed Solana hot prefix AdNFLxrs… with 371,874 USDT is not expanded to a full address in this draft. Tornado Cash activity continued into early January; a 5 Jan “stop” is not hashed here.

On-chain evidence

Merkle Science consolidators (full): 0x72c1eabafc42a2ac6d0447b02c657b96f07402e6 (ETH and BSC), 0x4b0edd27196063476d91b634333be289beca9202, 0x6c9b55b50e6a42fd7a14b49ba7747096090b0465.

Coverage gap. Solana hot wallet AdNFLxrs… is not recovered as a full pubkey. The 371,874 USDT Solana outflow set and a 5 Jan Tornado stop transaction are not hashed. Do not invent them.

Root cause

Solana bridge / hot-wallet withdrawal rules that allowed many small exits (smurfing) without an aggregate velocity halt.

Response

Delayed public admission (ZachXBT 24 Jan). Solana support paused, restored 11 Feb 2025 after pen-test claims. Hot-wallet float reduced.

Coverage gaps

  • Full Solana hot address AdNFLxrs….
  • 371,874 USDT transfer signatures.
  • 5 Jan Tornado stop hash.

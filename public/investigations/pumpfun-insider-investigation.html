Pump.fun insider

Wiki id pumpfun-insider-may-2024 · Live incident page

Summary

Former employee used withdraw authority plus MarginFi flash loans to complete bonding curves and take ~12.3k SOL (~$1.9M) between 15:21 and 17:00 UTC on 16 May 2024. Co-signer 7ihN8QaTfNoDTRTQGULCzbUT3PHwPDTu5Brcu4iT2paP; program 6EF8rrecthR5Dkzon8Nwu78hRvfCKubJ14M5uBEwF6P. Team halted trading and said it would replenish LPs to the 15:21 UTC mark.

On-chain evidence

Program 6EF8rrecthR5Dkzon8Nwu78hRvfCKubJ14M5uBEwF6P. Co-signer / attacker 7ihN8QaTfNoDTRTQGULCzbUT3PHwPDTu5Brcu4iT2paP. Current on-chain withdraw_authority 39azUYFWPz3VHgKCf3VChUwbpURdCHRxjWVowf5jUJjg is post-incident and is not substituted for the May 2024 key.

Coverage gap. Withdraw authority prefix 5PXxuZkv… is not recovered as a full pubkey. Replenish / seed-LP transaction hashes after the halt are not listed. Do not use the current withdraw authority as the insider key.

Root cause

Single-employee control of withdraw authority plus flash-loan completable bonding curves (insider, not an external 0-day in the program math alone).

Response

Trading halt ~17:00 UTC, contract upgrade, 0% fees for seven days, team-seeded LPs. Later UK prosecution of Jarett Dunn.

Coverage gaps

  • Full withdraw-authority pubkey 5PXxuZkv….
  • Replenish seed transaction hashes.

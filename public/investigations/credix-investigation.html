4 Aug 2025

CrediX (Sonic, not Solana CRDx)

Wiki id credix-rug-aug-2025 · Live incident page

Chain correction. This incident is Sonic CrediX_fi, not the Solana Credix program CRDx2YkdtYtGZXGHZ59wNv1EwKHQndnRc1gT4p8i2vPX. Wiki pages that file the $4.5M drain under Solana application exploits mix two different products. Omit this drain from Solana program-loss totals.

Summary

On Sonic, admin EOA 0x0dd010513F7abB8F9c628dC164a24D953BCA09Cf granted attacker 0xF321683831Be16eeD74dfA58b02a37483cEC662e POOL_ADMIN, BRIDGE, ASSET_LISTING_ADMIN, EMERGENCY_ADMIN, and RISK_ADMIN via ACLManager on 29 Jul 2025. On 4 Aug 2025 the attacker called mintUnbacked and borrowed pool liquidity (~$4.5M reporting). The team later vanished after promising reimbursement (exit-scam reporting).

On-chain evidence

ACL role grant (29 Jul 2025): 0x0cc3520951a2b41281dcc9a0d37ef3f7f139b75675d83ae72e3b8e903334f35e.

mintUnbacked (4 Aug 2025 02:27 UTC, Sonic): 0xe2501b4bb580b2ff6e59e68c0de50fd716bf2d096e1647c70f826bbd1352624d — attacker 0xF321683831Be16eeD74dfA58b02a37483cEC662e.

Victim pools cited by QuillAudits: 0x0850A9759165B25832E2cAa3dB3f2d04dc583D4E, 0x56eb1bcB2aA011517fD7bf32641E79Bd8471770e.

Coverage gap. The borrow cited as 0x71301581… for 225,000.000164 USDC is not expanded to a full hash in this draft. Do not invent the remaining hex. QuillAudits lists borrowed notionals (USDC ~$2.04M, scUSD ~$1.16M, wS ~$1.34M, stS ~$56k, WETH ~$46k) without that specific 225,000.000164 USDC signature.

Root cause

Concentrated ACL roles, including BRIDGE mintUnbacked, on an EOA. Not a Solana program bug and not Credix Finance CRDx2Ykd….

Response

Front end taken down; reimbursement promised then abandoned. Team socials went dark around 8 Aug 2025. Stability DAO later coordinated legal follow-up.

Coverage gaps

  • Borrow tx prefix 0x71301581… (225,000.000164 USDC) — full hash missing.
  • Wiki incident still titled as if it were Solana Credix; this draft is the correction.

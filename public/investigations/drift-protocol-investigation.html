1 Apr 2026

Drift Protocol durable-nonce / fake-CVT

Wiki id drift-protocol-apr-2026 · Live incident page

Summary

On 1 Apr 2026 ~16:05 UTC, two pre-signed durable-nonce Squads v4 transactions transferred Drift admin to a plain wallet, then fake CarbonVote Token (CVT) collateral and raised withdraw guards enabled borrowing of real assets. Public reporting ~$285M. Social-engineering of signers plus a zero-timelock 2-of-5 council, not a single program math bug.

On-chain evidence

Root cause

Durable-nonce pre-signs that could not be revoked without advancing the nonce; 2-of-5 council with zero timelock after a Mar 25 Squads migration; attacker-controlled oracle + fake CVT collateral.

Response

Industry reporting attributed the campaign to DPRK-linked groups.

WAX Multi-Chain Bridge

Wiki id wax-multi-chain-bridge-oct-2026 · Live incident page

Summary

On 22 Oct 2026 the WAX Multi-Chain Bridge released funds on Solana and Ethereum. Solana-side loss is USDC only: 52,986.497732 USDC at 2026-10-22T10:00:55Z from vault 8cqv3kRg1ivwPNrGGrvJANXb6RPik6XEhnBzs18tPcKC to attacker 4H1piNZ8LsYhZGYhkbJDbPDSghxRj3HWxJqVz5dve8Q7. Ethereum then released USDT, DAI, WETH, and WBTC to 0x01664E199991b2F3fe412c2DE5B9b7A0dcDc5070. Destination chains released after the source was reported to have charged nothing. USD totals in public posts are not re-priced here.

Root cause (reported)

Reported, not WAX-chain verified here: WAX eth.token::withdraw accepted max_fee that did not satisfy max_fee ≥ 0 (reported as max_fee = -quantity), so the source charged nothing. Destination chains (Solana/Ethereum) still released. Lesson: destination release must not honor a source withdraw that paid no fee. Pointers: SlowMist, WAX, Blockaid — not a substitute for pulling WAX withdraw params.

Response

Public pointers from SlowMist, WAX, and Arisk/Blockaid on 22 Oct 2026. No hashed pause, patch, or recovery transaction is included in this draft.

Stake Nova RedeemNovaSol flash-loan

Wiki id stake-nova-flash-loan-feb-2026 · Live incident page

Summary

SlowMist and the wiki date this as 27 Feb 2026. Stake Nova reported an unchecked validation issue in RedeemNovaSol that a flash loan used to drain the community / liquidity pool. Team figures: 1,563.2 SOL (~$137k; SlowMist $137,014, ~95% of deposits). White-hat offer: return 1,406.88 SOL, keep 156.32 SOL bounty.

On-chain evidence

Coverage gap. The RedeemNovaSol flash-loan signature is not in the public sources used here, so the on-chain date cannot be independently confirmed against the wiki date of 27 Feb 2026. Remaining SOL on an attacker seed is also unhashed. Do not invent either.

Root cause

Business-logic / validation flaw on the liquid-staking redeem path under flash-loan composition (SlowMist: RedeemNovaSol()). Team associated it with a SKR rewards upgrade.

Response

Patch, dApp taken offline, 10% on-chain bounty offer. Chris Dior (X) is the wiki’s linked contemporaneous post.

  • Attacker seed remaining-SOL balance (no hash).

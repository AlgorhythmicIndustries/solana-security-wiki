30 Sep 2026

TradeWiz

Wiki id tradewiz-key-export-sep-2026 · Live incident page

Bug class. Product/ops private-key exposure (custodial signing) with multi-user fund loss on Solana. Official posts and Bitquery treat this as key exposure, not a protocol exploit and not one-off phishing. TradeWiz has not published how keys left the SOL PVP export feature; this draft does not infer a leak path.

Summary

TradeWiz (@TradeWiz_Bot) posted on 30 Sep 2026 that a limited private-key exposure occurred in the SOL PVP private-key export feature. Mass wallet drains followed. The bot holds user keys so it can sign. Bitquery’s public write-up says the on-chain artifact is ordinary transfers signed by the drained wallets, with no exploit program and no token approval.

Multi-user impact remains a Bitquery claim except where hashed below: Bitquery counts 20,933 wallets and about $459,468 at $120/SOL as of 1 Oct 2026 09:54 UTC. TradeWiz has not published a refund or police dollar figure in the posts reviewed here. RPC check of collector 6mmiAPpQmY6YFxecmAyHMi2P8B7CE92Y8E65saE388Hj on 3 Oct 2026 ~11:50 ET: 1845.184891292 SOL, with mass activity 30 Sep 09:00–13:00 ET and 1 Oct 06:00–11:00 ET.

28 Sep test window

28 Sep 2026 14:40–14:45 ET: collector inflows over 0.01 SOL from at least 10 wallets summed 33.68 SOL. Same window as Bitquery’s 18-wallet ~34 SOL test. This pass does not assert exactly 18 wallets.

Still Bitquery-only (not independently recounted here): 20,933 wallets; 20,776-wallet / 3,488.6 SOL four-run table; ~73% of main-sweep SOL after the bot went quiet; ~93k token accounts / ~189 SOL rent; two wallets emptied again within 4 seconds after new deposits; 27 TradeWiz trades on the trading wallet; $459,468 book at $120/SOL (3,523.29 SOL swept, 189.31 SOL rent, 7,373.40 USDC, 1,534.16 USDT, 15.59 WSOL, other tokens booked as 3,176.54 CASH); median 63 cents; 69 wallets ≥10 SOL. Bitquery does not know who signed the drains or how keys left the export feature.

Root cause

Official claim: limited private-key exposure in the SOL PVP private-key export feature. Product model: TradeWiz holds user keys to sign. How those keys left the export feature is unpublished. This draft does not speculate a leak path.

Response

Official @TradeWiz_Bot posts (X API timestamps; long text via note_tweet where present):

  • 30 Sep 2026 14:41 UTC — security notice: limited SOL PVP export exposure; full compensation promised; stop using existing SOL PVP addresses. SOL Bot, Robin PVP, and BSC PVP said not affected and given additional upgrades.
  • 30 Sep 2026 18:08 UTC — first-wave refunds sent; verified losses to be fully refunded; EVM services paused as a precaution; claims need verification.
  • 1 Oct 2026 07:34 UTC — police report and exchange tracing; identity/KYC steps claimed via authorities; attacker return-and-drop-legal offer.
  • 2 Oct 2026 02:16 UTC (1 Oct ~10:16 PM ET) — wallet-risk reminder: any wallet previously associated with TradeWiz may be at risk; stop using old associated addresses; losses from continued use of an old associated address after this announcement are not eligible for compensation.
  • 3 Oct 2026 03:13 UTC (2 Oct ~11:13 PM ET) — Robinhood/RBH notice: caption says Robinhood (RBH) users’ wallet addresses and funds were not affected; they still reviewed and upgraded. Attached graphic: additional security upgrade completed; service restoration 02:30 UTC. The graphic does not print a calendar date. Restoration is an official claim, not independently confirmed here.

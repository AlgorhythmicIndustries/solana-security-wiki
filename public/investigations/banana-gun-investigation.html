Banana Gun

Wiki id banana-gun-sep-2024 · Live incident page

Summary

Banana Gun Telegram bot incident on Ethereum. Public figures include ~715.5 ETH across a collector set (QuillAudits 11 wallets plus two early Quadriga-linked addresses = 13). No Solana collector is attached. Users were later refunded.

Root cause

Response

Bots paused; treasury refunds (Cointelegraph: ~$3M refund reporting).

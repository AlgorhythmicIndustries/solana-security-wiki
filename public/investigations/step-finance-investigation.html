31 Jan 2026

Step Finance treasury

Wiki id step-finance-treasury-jan-2026 · Live incident page

Summary

Executive-device compromise (not a public program bug) let the attacker change stake + withdrawer authority, then withdraw ~261,932.41 SOL from treasury stake. Wiki $27–40M variance is mark/timing, not a second hashed drain.

Coverage gap. This draft stops at the hashed SOL withdrawal. Mayan / CCTP hops after LEP1uHXcWbFEPwQgkeFzdhW2ykgZY6e9Dz8Yro6SdNu are not hashed here. The 28 Jan 0.2 SOL gas fund from treasury to the exploiter is also unhashed.

Root cause

Endpoint compromise on executive devices holding stake authority. Native Stake Program instructions were valid once authority moved.

Response

Public disclosure 31 Jan; STEP crashed. Partial Remora / other recoveries reported. Step, SolanaFloor, and Remora later wound down with a pre-incident snapshot for token-holder treatment.

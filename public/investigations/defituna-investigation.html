16 Jul 2026

DeFiTuna lending

Wiki id defituna-lending-jul-2026 · Live incident page

Summary

On 16 Jul 2026 ~05:48 UTC, attackers opened a zero-collateral DeFiTuna spot position, borrowed ~570k USDC, and routed 569,601 USDC through an attacker-seeded illiquid TUNA/USDC Fusion pool. Position valuation rounded to zero, the health check treated the loan as healthy, and the USDC sat in two attacker limit-order positions (~284,280.483231 USDC each).

On-chain evidence

Program tuna4uSQZncNeeiAMKbstuxA9CUkHH6HmC64wgmnogD; damaged USDC vault D76dDcSU5HnAGqVEZCDLyGgLpTp4xZuqeZyVDtUdDv55; fake pool also cited as 917DKTphW3rhBG5gsJpwKsNGisNV2dx74uUFd8HBEjtg.

Limit-order owners (CertiK): 7hiHL8AgDuLNVDQLfN3GHdLAEeCN1F7uz6nSANRvFJst and BK9aTnKfPNnnj45Me5ACrky2vexzUrZHRzr4BjmQpH3c. Additional attacker labels: 9ytGWP8tCRF1keREJ5VHqBpSuM9MZYwm3oFQQa1SvESb, 8P3H7Hy98LWhw5QhuXdoigVdAzPCYRTYjdtQQKhGwvqD.

CertiK: those two limit-order wallets bridged Solana → Ethereum via Mayan. This draft stops at Mayan.

Coverage gap. BlockSec cites exploit prefix 4x33Dq…EXj1 without expanding the full signature. Mayan bridge signatures from the two Solana wallets are not hashed here. Do not invent post-Mayan hops (Railgun, DAI leftovers, or EVM mixers).

Root cause

is_healthy treated total == 0 as an empty healthy position even with outstanding debt. Caller-supplied Jupiter routes were not bound to the oracle-checked pool.

Response

Team said the vector was identified and mitigated the same day and that they were attempting recovery. Wiki still lists ~$580k estimated loss.

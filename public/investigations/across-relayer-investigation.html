Across Protocol Solana relayer

Wiki id across-protocol-solana-relayer-jul-2026 · Live incident page

Summary

At ~05:30 UTC on 17 Jul 2026, Across’s Solana spoke was attacked. Losses were limited to the Risk Labs relayer that fronts capital in the intent model. User funds were not pooled; in-flight fills completed. Later Across reporting put gross relayer payout near $4.5M across 581 filled forgeries (1,627 forged deposits).

On-chain evidence

Live signer IoC is 8bkoZToaTBBtAPczgHqD4XVxWtvBkiy4crtexEtYDYSL — note the o in 8bkoZToa. Some early coverage (Crypto Times) printed the typo 8bkoZTaTBBtAPczgHqD4XVxWtvBkiy4crtexEtYDYSL (missing o). Do not use the typo as an IoC.

EVM addresses published with the Solana signer: 0xa0C0e9f307b5A26cA3FB5891c19154fc7A02BeF7, 0xA6fb971F3B7a9b9F76EdA76bc89268fe26560189.

Coverage gap. An Ethereum USDC floor of ~1.2M is not hashed in this draft. Do not invent that mint or transfer hash. Forged Solana deposit signatures (1,627 single-use wallets) are also not enumerated here.

Root cause

Off-chain relayer event parsing (SvmCpiEventsClient) accepted inner instructions through the SpokePool event-authority PDA without checking the 8-byte Anchor event discriminator. Attackers CPI’d a read-only helper with forged FundsDeposited payloads. No user escrow moved.

Response

Solana deposits disabled within minutes. SEAL 911 tracing. Later post-mortem: CCTP fallback for Solana order flow; white-hat recovery reporting followed. Not a protocol vault drain.

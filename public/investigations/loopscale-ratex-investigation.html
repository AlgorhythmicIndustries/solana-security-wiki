26 Apr 2025

Loopscale RateX

Wiki id loopscale-oracle-apr-2025 · Live incident page

Summary

Attacker inflated RateX principal-token marks, then CREATE_LOAN against Loopscale vaults. Five hashed loans: four USDC (1.5M + 1.5M + 1.5M + 1,226,725) and 1,211.4 WSOL. Funds returned 27–29 Apr 2025 after negotiation.

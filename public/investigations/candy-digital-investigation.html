11 Oct 2026

Candy Digital

Wiki id candy-digital-custodial-oct-2026 · Live incident page

Bug class. Product/ops custodial key exposure with multi-user fund loss on Solana. Candy generated and held the keys for users’ Phantom-based wallets, so the on-chain pattern looks like a bulk key leak. This draft does not infer how those keys left Candy’s custody. Drains are ordinary system transfers signed by the victim wallets: no approval and no drainer program.

Summary

Collector HLwZCJB8YU6Yvq8HNMMNNrLCo1xammz95bCa3jm2k75c received 425.991874 SOL across 1,549 inflows from 1,549 unique senders on 11–13 Oct 2026 UTC. Six of those inflows are address-poisoning dust of 0.00001 SOL or less. Real victims are 1,543 wallets / 425.99186 SOL. Public reports that say 1,543 versus 1,549 wallets are counting real victims versus all senders.

Wave 1 (11 Oct 23:02:44–23:10:31 UTC) moved 149.39 SOL from 12 real victims, plus one dust transfer. Candy’s lockdown was reported at 00:22 UTC on 12 Oct, between the waves. Wave 2 (12 Oct 09:55:17–11:22:57 UTC) moved 276.60 SOL from 1,531 real victims. At 23:58:22 UTC on 12 Oct the collector sent 420 SOL to FvNJKwKcVJahVCbKE6JFuic59FRWGqZBt3xgeVp9TFcz. No CEX touch has been seen. No NFTs or USDC were taken in this collector flow.

Root cause

Unconfirmed. Candy generated and held the keys for users’ Phantom-based wallets, so the pattern looks like a bulk key leak. This draft does not infer a leak path.

Response

Official Candy community update (12 Oct 2026): a security protocol locks minted NFTs (no transfers or sales between wallets) until Candy is satisfied the platform and user control can be reestablished; two internal audits had given Candy no reason to believe NFTs were lost; Candy said it was preparing to reimburse drained Solana and to communicate platform-reboot plans. Candy stated it will never ask for a seed phrase or private key.

Public reports place the lockdown announcement at 00:22 UTC on 12 Oct 2026, between the waves. That timestamp is a pointer claim, not an on-chain event. Reimbursement is an official promise and is not hashed here.

1–5 Nov 2026

Loopscale Genesis Vault exploits

Wiki id loopscale-genesis-vault-nov-2026 · Live incident page

Summary

Two distinct attacks hit Loopscale Genesis vaults on program 1oopBoJG58DgkUVKkEzKgyG9dvRmpgeEm1AVjoHkF78 on 1 Nov and 5 Nov 2026. They are not the April 2025 RateX oracle incident and they are not one bug. Chain reconstruction was read ~2026-11-05T13:00Z and re-checked ~23:00Z the same day. Times are UTC ISO.

Vector 1 — SOL Genesis vault, 1 Nov 2026. At 2026-11-01T19:41:05Z slot 464213236, 247Lk5YNH9URfBBvxVUtDruz8GvGX8gKGuYHVCZThpiR plus Loopscale co-signer CyNKPfqsSLAejjZtEeNG3pR4SkPhSPHXdGhuNTyudrNs ran CreateLoan, DepositCollateral, UpdateWeightMatrix, and BorrowPrincipal in mZCoHzevv7nrPsSJDjnGZVRQMeUvuwQ2BuPUeCWhCYBheaWjoRkETU6Jh6zb2o2bgrhRAA4axy2QNQtpDZNFsmD. One kySOL (9 decimals), bought for 1.310991880 SOL at 19:38:31Z, was valued using a zBTC (8 decimals) identity. Vault wSOL 8oCX3kkgviLPxYGBfSt1kCKWVmHer1pHqutPTiBYnGpu fell 4,597 SOL (5,719.566856263 → 1,122.566856263). Extraction was 4,596 SOL plus 1.677556797 SOL to H1TEUnWaEmrUrp4xKykYmrZCPK8z5HYryv8RhvPFSeiJ, then Relay to ~190.22 ETH at 0xB7Ec7eF24FD73f3000C651CA9D0Fa45b61DcF854. The drain signer is 247Lk, not AzPh.

Vector 2 — USDC Genesis vault, 5 Nov 2026. AzPh2dUV3duK9n8PWV6CxKMwWr9Rk7fxPNBZuv1CjwMr created its own strategy, borrowed 120 USDC against 2 wSOL, deposited 65,000 USDC for 58,326.202736 shares, set a strategy rate field to 0xfffffffffffffffe, refinanced and sold the ledger to vault 6tv6XYnXUFjLSaAeap4T1GQusYeEw3nK6KTcVyyHf3ns for 120.000071 USDC, then burned 465.264135 shares for 586,000 USDC at 06:09:33Z (net 521,120 USDC). Loopscale published AzPh…CiwMr; that address has no history. The real signer is …CjwMr.

A Squads-gated upgrade at 2026-11-04T04:52:42Z preceded vector 2. No later program-data upgrade was seen through ~23:00Z Nov 5. That check does not claim the program is unpatched today. SOL make-whole is hashed on 3 Nov. No USDC make-whole was visible on the vault token account at the Nov 5 check.

Vector 1 vault loss: 4,597 SOL from SOL Genesis vault wSOL ATA 8oCX3kkgviLPxYGBfSt1kCKWVmHer1pHqutPTiBYnGpu (owner path via EPneH88gcV1kKi1ykj96XmuJsrkbF9aL5iTwvv6eZjW9). Extracted 4,596 + 1.677556797 SOL. Cost of the 1 kySOL collateral: 1.310991880 SOL swap spend.

Root cause

Vector 1 (reported by r0bre)

Reported by r0bre (X article 2118373644887900519, 2026-11-05T16:03:29Z), not independently binary-diffed here: oracle_type == 19 (BeamAccount) early return Ok() in verify_collateral_data / state/oracle.rs skipped the asset-type check. That allowed an Orca label with the wrong mint and price identity. The exploitable on-chain effect, which is hashed, is a 9-decimal kySOL amount valued as 8-decimal zBTC. r0bre reports the bypass was introduced in the 18 Mar 2026 upgrade and removed in the 4 Nov 2026 upgrade (49-byte ELF diff; Aug 18 hash 00396af8… / Nov 4 hash 33b6e389…; “four lines”; “228 days”; audit history). Those ELF and audit claims stay reported.

r0bre also reports that CyNKPf is Loopscale’s production BS_AUTH co-signer and infers an API policy bypass rather than a stolen key. Chain shows CyNKPf signed the drain. Chain does not show the key was stolen, and this entry does not assert that it was.

Vector 2 (inference)

Mechanism: an uncapped strategy rate (0xfffffffffffffffe) inflated vault book value so burning 465.264135 of 58,326.202736 shares redeemed 586,000 USDC. That is our inference, medium-high confidence, not an official root cause. Loopscale’s 6 Nov statement described selling a loan with an extreme interest rate into the USDC Genesis Vault and inflating NAV (~$521k) — reported, and consistent with the hashed steps, but not independently confirmed as the same internal accounting identity. The 4 Nov patch is not treated as a fix for this vector.

Lessons

A lending vault can take two unrelated accounting hits in one week. Closing an oracle-type early return does not prove strategy-rate and share-pricing paths are safe. A mandatory co-signer that will sign whatever the program would accept is not an independent control. A depositor-whole statement should be reconcilable against the drained token account, or the gap should be stated.

Texture lending

Wiki id texture-lending-jul-2025 · Live incident page

Summary

Texture vault rebalance lacked an ownership check on the LP destination. Two drain transactions on 9 Jul 2025 credited 6DpG2dUoYGJrhTTC5bTHbyuQ3SGxpDRjqZzwduwNJY1k a unique 2,208,490.894823 USDC. The wiki’s $220k figure is the 10% bounty kept after ~$1.98M was returned, not the unique drain.

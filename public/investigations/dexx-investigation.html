DEXX

Wiki id dexx-key-leak-nov-2024 · Live incident page

Summary

DEXX custodial key mishandling led to mass wallet drains (Solana and other chains), later laundered in part via Tornado Cash and cross-chain hops including Layerswap in community tracing. MistTrack named EVM consolidator 0xFFB9926310b291E17D7Df846cdED66cDA2C68228 and a Solana collector prefix 4smi8TTE…CHLd.

On-chain evidence

EVM consolidator 0xFFB9926310b291E17D7Df846cdED66cDA2C68228 (full). MistTrack also cited a second Solana prefix GPuNX…RKgt.

Coverage gap. Solana collector 4smi8TTE… is not recovered as a full pubkey. ETH Tornado deposit hashes and Layerswap stop hashes are not listed. Do not invent them.

Root cause

Centralized storage of user private keys for a Telegram-style trading product; operator admitted the leak was their responsibility.

Response

Service warnings, SlowMist/MistTrack tracing, promised compensation program. Recovery of stolen principal is not hashed here.

Coverage gaps

  • Full Solana collector 4smi8TTE…CHLd.
  • Tornado / Layerswap stop transaction hashes.
